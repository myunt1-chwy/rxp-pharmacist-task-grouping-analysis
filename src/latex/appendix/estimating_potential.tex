\subsection{Estimating the Potential of Grouping Similar Tasks}
Figures~\ref{fig:dur-partition-sankey} and
\ref{fig:dur-processing-time-sankey} summarize DUR task counts and imputed
processing time, respectively, from all tasks through the 3,433,218-task
model sample and its \texttt{SAME\_PRECEDING} groups. The processing-time
cutoff is 156.608019 seconds. ``Possible multitasking'' means a DUR
overlaps another task for the same user; that other task need not be a DUR.
``Singletasking'' means no such overlap was recorded. The 54,138 other
exclusions are the difference between the non-overlapping, no-handoff
population and valid DURs; their individual exclusion reasons have not
been broken out. The 180,803 valid DURs at or above the cutoff account
for 24,710.67 hours, nearly the same as the 24,758.52 hours among the
3,433,218 valid DURs below it.

The monetary scenario excludes these above-cutoff tasks (9) because
their long recorded dwell may include multitasking or other delays;
and we assume the pharmacist won't benefit from doing the
same task repeatedly.

Region 12 is used to estimate the potential gain. We assume that the
DUR tasks of a pharmacist can be rearranged for a given day. We also
assume that the pharmacist does not multitask or encounter a DUR
failure during stretches of successful DURs to that break the streak.

Figure~\ref{fig:same-preceding-counterfactual-flow}
shows the calculation: within each user, process date, and cohort, the first
task keeps its observed duration and later tasks with
\texttt{SAME\_PRECEDING = 0} receive the reduced duration obtained from the model.

Table~\ref{tab:same-preceding-counterfactual-beta-bounds} varies only the
\texttt{SAME\_PRECEDING} coefficient over its fitted 95\% confidence interval.
Each row uses the same 1,814,117 adjusted tasks and \$90 hourly rate.
The source extract includes tasks created from 2026-02-01 inclusive through
2026-08-01 exclusive, a 181-day window. Retained process dates extend to
August 1, making their inclusive date span 182 days, but August 1 is not an
additional full source day. Annualized values multiply the period values by
$365/181$, assuming the same average daily volume and task mix throughout the year.

fitted association. The coefficient bounds do not form a confidence interval
for realizable cash savings, which would also depend on staffing decisions.
