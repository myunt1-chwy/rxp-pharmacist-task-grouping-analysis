\subsection{ Visual Summary of Factors Affecting DUR  }
This section contains a visual summary of the factors that affect DUR duration. The goal is to be exhaustive in detail knowing well that
some of the factors cannot be measured.
Figure~\ref{fig:dur-detailed-causal-graph} shows the hypothesized relationships. This graph guides the exploratory data analysis and the final model structure. The main factors affecting DUR duration are the pharmacist, the short and long term experience of the pharmacist (Pharmacist Past in the figure), the task itself containing; the prescription the pet, the product and other metadata, the software platform (Oracle or RxBuddy), time of day, time of the week, seasonality, management/process regulations  and external disturbances such as inbound calls.

\subsection{ Select Factors Affecting DUR Duration}\label{factors-affecting-successful-durs}
We carried out extensive exploratory data analysis to understand the factors affecting DUR duration. The following sections contain the more prominent factors encountered.
The causal graph section contains a visual summary of the hypothesized mechanisms that affect DUR duration.
\subsubsection{Pharmacist Performance}
Pharmacists vary in their speed to process DURs (Figure~\ref{fig:user-median-dur-distributions}). In addition, if a pharmacist is fast in handling a cohort 1 DUR, highly likely he/she is also fast handling other cohort DURs. Figure~\ref{fig:user-median-all-cohorts} shows the correlation of DUR median duration of  120 people who have carried out at least 30 task per cohort. As can be seen the performances correlate positively.
